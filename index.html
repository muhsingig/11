04 · How it works

Three steps between you and the driver's seat. None of them involve a queue.

  1. i.

    Choose the car

    Pick a model, not a category. The car in the photo is the car that arrives.

  2. ii.

    We bring it to you

    Home, hotel, office or the arrivals curb at JFK. Keys in hand within the hour.

  3. iii.

    Leave it anywhere

    Done driving? Send us a pin. We collect it, refuel or recharge it and handle the rest.
